Solo project

ProofLine

AI resume-JD matching and factuality verification

ProofLine is an AI-powered system that analyzes resumes against job descriptions, identifies skill gaps, and generates evidence-based resume rewrites. I also built an independent factuality guardrail that breaks generated rewrites into individual claims and verifies them against the original resume, helping prevent fabricated experience or inflated claims.

My role
Designed and built the backend and AI pipeline, including the semantic matching and factuality-verification workflows.
Outcome
Validated the factuality guardrail on a 34-case adversarial dataset, achieving 100% precision and 100% recall on that fixed evaluation set.
